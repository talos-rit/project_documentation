## Script

- CHRISTINE, IN MEDIA RES: And this is the part that'll be on the exam.
- [Christine walks out of frame. The camera does not move.]
- ALECK, WALKING INTO FRAME: Most lectures are recorded by a camera that can't move. So when the professor *does* move, you get this.
- ALECK: Real productions fix this with camera operators and a director. But no school can put a film crew in every classroom.
- ALECK: But what if you could turn an old crummy clanker into an automated film crew on a budget?
- [split-screen shot of bluey and bingo synced to Aleck saying their names. maybe super smash bros matchup music?]
- ALECK: Meet Bluey and Bingo, two forty-year-old robot arms. We've given them new life, using computer vision to track the speaker and frame the shot in the way a real camera operator would.
- ALECK: So professors can focus on teaching, students get a lecture worth watching, and old hardware gains a new purpose—saved from the e-waste bin. And, because Talos is open-source, any classroom can use it, and people can adapt it to suit their needs.
	- [During monologue, show B-roll/stock photos of teachers, remote learners, and the robots, then finally our Github team page]
- [A-Roll shot #2: Show Commander UI on projector screen, Aleck pointing at it.]
- ALECK: This is Talos. Simple, easy to use. Just press this button and it starts tracking.
- [Cut back to earlier shot. Christine walks across the room again. This time the camera follows her ((because someone turned on Auto-Track in Commander))]
- ALECK: Oh, and this whole video?
- [Cut to Commander UI showing this exact same shot in its frame.]
- CHRISTINE: Filmed by Talos.
- [final shot (not filmed on Talos) of both the robots together. this is a shot of someone filming the robots filming Christine & Aleck. it's a super meta thing.]
- [Black screen with Talos logo saying "Shot on Talos" in SF Pro font]
