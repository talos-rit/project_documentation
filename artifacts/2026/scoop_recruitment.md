Adding a technical intern will allow us to accomplish more hardware goals. We have extensive onboarding documentation that outlines exactly what is needed to get started with our project. The first week or so, we would help the intern get onboarded to all necessary aspects of the project. After this, the intern would mainly work with Aleck and Jacob on hardware and embedded tasks, but still collaborate with the whole team.
